Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how My Personal App ("the Application") handles information. The Application is strictly a private, personal project created for individual use exclusively by the developer. It is not offered as a public service or accessible to third-party users.

2. Data Collection, Storage, and Processing

The Application accesses Google user data via OAuth strictly to perform automated workflows initiated by the developer. Data processing involves:

  • Data Download & Processing: The Application downloads and analyzes requested Google data solely to perform core processing tasks and personal automation.
  • Data Storage: Downloaded data may be stored locally or within secure private infrastructure strictly for processing, analysis, and application functionality.
  • Access Control: All processed and stored data is accessible exclusively by the developer. No other individuals or external users have access to this application or its data.

3. Use of Google API Data

The Application's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Service User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

No personal data, downloaded files, or Google user data is ever shared, rented, transferred, or sold to third parties, advertisers, or external marketing platforms.

5. Contact Information

This Application is maintained for private use. If you have any inquiries regarding this Application or its Privacy Policy, please reach out directly through the support channel provided in the Google OAuth Consent Screen.
